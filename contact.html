Frequently Asked

How long does hand-finishing take?

Most pieces are made in numbered runs of 40 or fewer; restocks typically follow within 4–6 weeks because nothing is pre-cut.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — worldwide from New Delhi and Paris, with complimentary shipping over $150 and duties calculated at checkout where applicable.

What's your returns policy?

Unworn, tagged pieces may be returned within 7 days of delivery in India (30 days internationally) for a full refund.

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Yes, on select pincodes across India. Prepaid orders unlock complimentary shipping and priority dispatch.

Can I book a private fitting?

Absolutely — 45-minute sessions at the New Delhi flagship or Paris atelier. Email ahead and we'll reserve a room.

Do you provide a GST invoice?

Yes — a GST-compliant invoice is generated automatically with every Indian order and emailed on dispatch.

Book a Private Fitting

A fitting at your pace. Forty-five unhurried minutes with a fitter, the season's pieces laid out, and honest advice on cut, cloth and care.
