Chapter One — The Maison

A house built
on patience.

Maison Éclat is a small, independent couture house working between two cities — Paris and Jaipur. We make clothes the slow way, because we have never found a faster one that produced something worth keeping.

Est. 2016 Paris × Jaipur 40+ Artisan Partners Shipping Worldwide
Courtyard Studio 6th arrondissement, Paris — where the house began
Since 2016 — A quiet beginning

Who we are, and why we are slow on purpose.

Maison Éclat began as a single rack of coats in a Paris courtyard studio — twelve pieces, each finished by one pair of hands. A decade on, we are still small by choice. Every piece is pattern-cut, sewn and finished by hand before it ever reaches a shelf, which means we make fewer clothes, not more.

Our Paris atelier sits in the 6th arrondissement, working alongside long-standing craft partners in Jaipur for embroidery and leatherwork. It is a quiet collaboration between two cities that take handwork equally seriously — and a refusal to treat any garment as disposable.

Camille Rousseau Founder & Creative Director

A Decade in Thread

Ten years, told slowly.

Every chapter of the house, from a single rack to two cities of craft.

2016

A courtyard, twelve coats

Camille Rousseau opens a small studio in the 6th arrondissement of Paris. The first collection — twelve hand-finished coats — sells out in nine days through word of mouth alone.

Paris, France
2018

The Jaipur partnership

A first meeting in Jaipur with a family of embroiderers leads to a long-term collaboration. Zari and resham work begins to appear in the collections, always by hand, always signed by the karigar.

Jaipur, India
2020

Numbered runs begin

The house commits to small batches — forty pieces or fewer per style. Each piece is hand-numbered and wax-sealed in the inner pocket. Nothing is reprinted; when a run sells, it is re-cut.

Paris × Jaipur
2022

The Fabric Library opens

Every fibre is traced and labelled — Banarasi mulberry silk, Kashmiri pashmina, Rajasthani vegetable-tanned leather. Customers receive a card detailing the origin of every cloth.

Maison Atelier
2024

Karigar Spotlight programme

Quarterly profiles of the artisan families behind each collection — Jaipur, Varanasi, Kutch, Kashmir — published in full with names, stories and signatures.

Across India
Today

Still small. Still slow.

Forty artisan partners, two ateliers, one uncompromising standard. We remain independent, privately held, and committed to making clothes that outlast the season they were bought in.

Worldwide
Our Manifesto

Three commitments,
no exceptions.

The principles we have never bent, and do not intend to.

01

Hand-Finished, Always

Every garment is finished by a named artisan whose hands you could shake — not a production line. Their signature is sewn inside, and their story is published in our Karigar Spotlight.

02

Made to Sell Out, Not Discount

We hold runs small enough to sell through fully, so nothing hand-finished ends up in landfill. A wait of four to six weeks for a restock is the price of integrity — and we pay it gladly.

03

Sourced, Not Just Sold

Fabric mills, dyers, tanners and embroiderers are visited in person before we sign a single order. We can tell you the name of the family who wove your silk, and we often do.
