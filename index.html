The Fabric Library

Know your cloth

We buy fibre the slow way — from mills and families we can name across India and France — and we write it into every label so you know exactly what you own.

Banarasi Mulberry Silk

Twenty-two momme crepe, woven on looms in Varanasi that run at half the speed of modern mills so the fibre keeps its liquid fall. Kettle-dyed by hand; no two baths are ever quite alike.

The Atelier

Every seam is a decision, not an accident.

We work with a small circle of ateliers in Paris — and pattern cutters and karigars in Jaipur, where hand-finishing with zari and resham has been a family trade for generations. A coat is cut once, fitted twice, and finished over days, not minutes.

When a run sells through, we do not reprint it; we re-cut it, which is why a restock takes four to six weeks and why the piece you receive is never a warehouse orphan.

The Éclat Method

Four doors before dispatch

01

Cut

Patterns are chalked by hand and cut once, single-ply, against the selvedge. Grain is checked twice; haste is checked at the door.

02

Fit

Each style is fitted on three bodies, not one mannequin — standing, sitting, reaching — before a single label is sewn.

03

Finish

Hems fell-stitched by hand, zari worked over silk, every bar-tack sealed. Eleven pairs of hands touch each piece.

04

Seal

Runs of forty or fewer. Your piece carries its number in ink and a wax seal in the inner pocket — a quiet signature.

Contact Us
Why do small batches take four to six weeks?

Because nothing is pre-cut. Cloth is ordered against your order, cut in a single lay, and finished by hand in sequence. The wait is the quality control.

How do I care for hand-finished pieces?

Rest wool between wears, steam rather than iron silk, and let leather dry away from heat. Every garment ships with a cloth-specific care card written by its finisher.

Do you ship worldwide, and what about duties?

Complimentary shipping over $150, dispatched tracked from Paris and Jaipur. Duties for EU and UK orders are prepaid at checkout; elsewhere they are calculated on delivery.

Do you provide a GST invoice?

Yes — a GST-compliant invoice is generated automatically with every order and emailed on dispatch.
